SSSIHMS ERP — Task Manual

Step-by-step instructions for 60 real ERP workflows across every module (HR, Facility Management & BMW, Trust/FCRA Compliance, Patient Ticketing, Patient Follow-Up, Sai Sparsh, Accounting, HRMS, Healthcare, Insights, Buzz, Education, LMS). 23 are illustrated with real screenshots from the 18-Aug-2026 end-to-end testing session in the Sai Hospital Smoke company; the rest are steps only, drawn from the live DocType configuration, where that session didn't happen to exercise the workflow — each says so rather than reusing an unrelated image. A second retest pass, same day, actually re-ran 25 of the steps-only workflows live and folded in what it found as "watch out for" notes — including one confirmed defect (AMC/CMC Warranty Contract can't currently be saved), one correction (Capital Purchase Requisition's Raised By is not auto-filled), and three workflows that correctly refuse to submit until a test company's GL setup is completed. Organized by task, not by module — find what you're trying to do. For the full field-by-field reference, see the companion User Manual.

HR (sssihms_hr)

Set up a Ward and assign a Roster

Get one ward on record and put an employee on a shift for a given day.

  1. HR workspace → Ward → New. Fill Ward Name (required, e.g. “ICU-2”), optionally Department, Floor, Nurse:Patient Ratio, Minimum Senior Coverage. Save.
  2. HR workspace → Roster Assignment → New. Pick Employee and Ward (both required).
  3. Enter Day as a number 0–6 (0=Monday … 6=Sunday) — required — and pick Shift: M/E/N/G (required).
  4. Leave Status as Draft until the roster is finalised, then change it to Published so staff can see it. Save.
Watch out for: Day = 0 (Monday) used to be silently rejected by a bug in sssihms_hr — fixed in production (commit e7d63e7). If a Monday roster row still refuses to save, that fix hasn't reached this site yet.
HR (sssihms_hr)

Request and approve a Shift Swap

Let an employee hand a shift to a colleague, with HR sign-off.

  1. Employee: Shift Swap Request → New. Pick yourself as From Employee (the system only lets a logged-in Employee submit a swap where they are the From Employee — picking someone else is rejected) and the colleague as To Employee.
  2. Fill Ward, Day of Month (rejected if out of range, e.g. 0 or above 31) and Shift (M/E/N/G).
  3. Add a Reason (optional), save with Status = Pending.
  4. HR Manager/User reviews it, sets Staffing Risk (Low/Medium/High), and changes Status to Approved or Rejected.

HR (sssihms_hr)

Log and verify a Staff Credential

Record a licence/registration for an employee and activate it.

  1. HR workspace → Staff Credential → New. Pick Employee and Credential Type (both required, e.g. “Nursing Council Registration”).
  2. Fill in Credential Number, Issuing Authority, Issue/Expiry Date if you have them, and attach the scanned certificate under Document.
  3. Save. Status will always save as “pending” — you cannot type “active” directly, even if you try.
  4. To actually activate it, open the saved record and run the Verify action from the menu (not a plain save). This is what sets Status to active and stamps Verified By / Verified At.
Watch out for: A plain edit can never set Status to active — only the Verify action can. This is deliberate, so activation is always attributable to whoever ran it.

Attach a Staff Document

File a private document (ID proof, contract) against an employee.

  1. Staff Document → New. Pick Employee, type a Category (free text, e.g. “ID Proof”), attach the File (all required).
  2. Set Verification Status (pending/verified/rejected) and save.
  3. To replace an outdated document, upload a new Staff Document with the same Category and link it via Supersedes — do not edit the old one in place.
Watch out for: The file is always stored privately, whatever attach method you use — there's no way to make it public from this form.

Raise a Capital Purchase Requisition (CPR)

Get a capital-equipment purchase approved through its full trail.

  1. Capital Purchase Requisition → New. Fill Raised By (pick yourself, or whoever is genuinely raising it), Department, Title, Estimated Value (all required) and a Justification.

Trust & FCRA Compliance

Record a Trust Donation

Post a donation to the correct fund with a gap-free receipt number.

  1. One-time setup: create the Fund (Fund Code/Name, Company, Fund Class — Corpus/Restricted/Designated/Unrestricted, and whether it's FCRA) and the Trust Donor (donor master with PAN, country, anonymous flag).
  2. Trust Donation → New. Pick Donor, Company, Donation Date, Amount and Mode (Cash/Bank/UPI/Cheque/In Kind).
  3. Pick the Fund this donation goes into. Tick Corpus Donation if the donor directed it to corpus, Grant if it's deferred-income grant money, Anonymous to suppress the donor's name on public reports.
  4. Leave Receipt No blank — it's auto-assigned from the gap-free receipt series. Save, then Submit — submitting is what posts the balanced GL entry and assigns the receipt number.
Watch out for: Get the Trust Company Account mapping (donation income, corpus fund, FCRA bank account, TDS) right before recording any donation — the posting logic uses it automatically and won't ask again mid-entry.

Patient Ticketing

Submit and handle a Patient Ticket

Let a member of the public raise a complaint/report, and have staff work it.

  1. Public, no login: on /submit-report, fill Patient Name, Mobile Number, Email Address (all required), optional UHID, and Department (required — the list is configured in Patient Ticket Settings). Attach any supporting files within the configured limits.
  2. The system creates the Patient Ticket itself on submit — there's no direct form save by the public. The submitter can check progress later at /ticket-status using the reference given at submission.
  3. Desk side (Patient Ticket Agent): move Status forward — Open → Under Review → Appointment Confirmed → Closed/Cancelled — add replies visible to the patient, and use Internal Notes for staff-only context.

Sai Sparsh

Log a Sai Sparsh visit and follow-up call

Run a lifestyle-coaching intake and a later coaching call against it.

  1. Healthcare → Sparsh Visit → New, filled once at the first visit: work top to bottom through the tabs — habits/conditions tables, the Curry (Nutrition) / Hurry (Movement) / Worry (Stress) sections, the measurements the caregiver consents to.

Accounting / ERPNext core

Raise a Sales Invoice

Bill a customer and post the GL entry in one step.

  1. Accounts → Sales Invoice → New. Pick the Company and Customer — billing address and currency fill in automatically.
  2. Add item rows with quantity and rate. Tax rows are pulled in automatically but can be adjusted.
  3. If the sale also moves stock and there's no separate Delivery Note, tick Update Stock so one document covers both.
  4. Save, then Submit. Submitting is what posts the GL entry — receivable, income and tax accounts update together; there's no separate “post to ledger” step.
Watch out for: An Item flagged Is Fixed Asset (e.g. a cath lab monitor tracked as a depreciable asset) is rejected on a Sales Invoice line unless you pick the specific Asset record it's billing against. For a normal sale, use an ordinary (non-fixed-asset) Item instead.

HRMS (Frappe HR)

Apply for and approve Leave

Submit a leave request and have it approved.

  1. HR → Leave Application → New. The form shows your current Allocated Leaves for reference.
  2. Pick the Leave Type and From/To dates. Save — there's no separate Submit step for the employee; saving changes the status to Open and emails your Leave Approver.
  3. As the Approver: you get an email when an application is saved; from the record, Approve, Reject or Cancel it — it's the Approver, not the employee, who does the final Submit.
Watch out for: A leave application can't span two different leave-allocation periods (split it into two applications), and it can't be submitted once payroll has already been processed for that period.

HRMS (Frappe HR)

Run Payroll
