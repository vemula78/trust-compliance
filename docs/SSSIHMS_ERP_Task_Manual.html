HR (sssihms_hr)

Set up a Ward and assign a Roster

Get one ward on record and put an employee on a shift for a given day.

  1. HR workspace → Ward → New. Fill Ward Name (required, e.g. “ICU-2”), optionally Department, Floor, Nurse:Patient Ratio, Minimum Senior Coverage. Save.
  2. HR workspace → Roster Assignment → New. Pick Employee and Ward (both required).
  3. Enter Day as a number 0–6 (0=Monday … 6=Sunday) — required — and pick Shift: M/E/N/G (required).
  4. Leave Status as Draft until the roster is finalised, then change it to Published so staff can see it. Save.
Watch out for: Day = 0 (Monday) used to be silently rejected by a bug in sssihms_hr — fixed in production (commit e7d63e7). If a Monday roster row still refuses to save, that fix hasn't reached this site yet.
HR (sssihms_hr)

Log and verify a Staff Credential

Record a licence/registration for an employee and activate it.

  1. HR workspace → Staff Credential → New. Pick Employee and Credential Type (both required, e.g. “Nursing Council Registration”).
  2. Fill in Credential Number, Issuing Authority, Issue/Expiry Date if you have them, and attach the scanned certificate under Document.
  3. Save. Status will always save as “pending” — you cannot type “active” directly, even if you try.
  4. To actually activate it, open the saved record and run the Verify action from the menu (not a plain save). This is what sets Status to active and stamps Verified By / Verified At.
Watch out for: A plain edit can never set Status to active — only the Verify action can. This is deliberate, so activation is always attributable to whoever ran it.
Accounting / ERPNext core

Raise a Sales Invoice

Bill a customer and post the GL entry in one step.

  1. Accounts → Sales Invoice → New. Pick the Company and Customer — billing address and currency fill in automatically.
  2. Add item rows with quantity and rate. Tax rows are pulled in automatically but can be adjusted.
  3. If the sale also moves stock and there's no separate Delivery Note, tick Update Stock so one document covers both.
  4. Save, then Submit. Submitting is what posts the GL entry — receivable, income and tax accounts update together; there's no separate “post to ledger” step.
Watch out for: An Item flagged Is Fixed Asset (e.g. a cath lab monitor tracked as a depreciable asset) is rejected on a Sales Invoice line unless you pick the specific Asset record it's billing against. For a normal sale, use an ordinary (non-fixed-asset) Item instead.
Accounting / ERPNext core

Raise a Purchase Invoice

Record a supplier bill.

  1. Accounts → Purchase Invoice → New. Pick Supplier.
  2. Add item or expense rows.
  3. Save, then Submit.
HRMS (Frappe HR)

Create an Employee record

Onboard a new staff member into HRMS.

  1. HR → Employee → New. Fill the required identity fields (name, date of joining, department, designation).
  2. Save — this is the master record everything else (leave, payroll, roster) links against.
HRMS (Frappe HR)

Apply for and approve Leave

Submit a leave request and have it approved.

  1. HR → Leave Application → New. The form shows your current Allocated Leaves for reference.
  2. Pick the Leave Type and From/To dates. Save — there's no separate Submit step for the employee; saving changes the status to Open and emails your Leave Approver.
  3. As the Approver: you get an email when an application is saved; from the record, Approve, Reject or Cancel it — it's the Approver, not the employee, who does the final Submit.
Watch out for: A leave application can't span two different leave-allocation periods (split it into two applications), and it can't be submitted once payroll has already been processed for that period.
Facility & Equipment Management

Register an Asset and its Preventive Maintenance schedule

Track a piece of equipment and when it's next due for servicing.

  1. Facility Management → Asset → New. Fill in the asset details (name, category, location).
  2. Facility Management → PM Schedule → New. Pick Reference Type = Asset and the specific asset record, Periodicity (Monthly/Quarterly/Half-Yearly/Annual) and Due Date.
  3. When the work is done: open or create the linked PM Record, fill Completion Date and who performed it, tick Has Certificate if one was received, and Submit — this rolls the PM Schedule's due date forward automatically.
Facility & Equipment Management

Log a Breakdown Repair Ticket

Track an equipment fault through to repair.

  1. Facility Management → Breakdown Repair Ticket → New. Pick the affected asset (Reference Type/Reference, required) and Priority (Low/Medium/High/Critical).
  2. Move Status through Open → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed in order as the repair progresses, filling the matching timestamp fields as each stage happens.
  3. Fill Root Cause and Corrective Action once resolved.
Facility & Equipment Management

Log Biomedical Waste (BMW) generation

Record a bag of waste generated by a department, ready for handover.

  1. Set up the generating department once: Facility Management → BMW Department → New — name, short Code, optional link to the HR Department.
  2. Each time a bag is generated: BMW Bag → New. Fill Bag No, Department, Category (Yellow/Red/White/Blue — the standard colour-coding), Weight (kg) and Generated At.
  3. If a bag was logged in error, set Status to Void with a Void Reason — do not delete it.
Watch out for: Is Cytotoxic only appears/matters when Category = Yellow.
Trust & FCRA Compliance

Record a Trust Donation

Post a donation to the correct fund with a gap-free receipt number.

  1. One-time setup: create the Fund (Fund Code/Name, Company, Fund Class — Corpus/Restricted/Designated/Unrestricted, and whether it's FCRA) and the Trust Donor (donor master with PAN, country, anonymous flag).
  2. Trust Donation → New. Pick Donor, Company, Donation Date, Amount and Mode (Cash/Bank/UPI/Cheque/In Kind).
  3. Pick the Fund this donation goes into. Tick Corpus Donation if the donor directed it to corpus, Grant if it's deferred-income grant money, Anonymous to suppress the donor's name on public reports.
  4. Leave Receipt No blank — it's auto-assigned from the gap-free receipt series. Save, then Submit — submitting is what posts the balanced GL entry and assigns the receipt number.
Watch out for: Get the Trust Company Account mapping (donation income, corpus fund, FCRA bank account, TDS) right before recording any donation — the posting logic uses it automatically and won't ask again mid-entry.
Healthcare

Book a Patient Appointment

Schedule a patient's visit to a practitioner.

  1. If the patient is new: Healthcare → Patient → New, then save.
  2. Healthcare → Patient Appointment → New. Pick the Medical Department, Practitioner and date — available slots for that practitioner are pulled from their schedule and shown with status indicators, so you pick an open slot rather than typing a time blind.
  3. Save. The appointment's own status then moves itself: Scheduled → Open (on the day) → Closed (once an Encounter or Clinical Procedure exists against it) — or Cancelled.
Watch out for: If an Appointment Type's own Default Duration is left blank/zero, any appointment booked against it keeps resetting its Duration to 0 whenever the Appointment Type field is touched — which then blocks saving with “Appointment end must be after start.” Fix this once, at the Appointment Type record, not per appointment.
Patient Ticketing

Submit and handle a Patient Ticket

Let a member of the public raise a complaint/report, and have staff work it.

  1. Public, no login: on /submit-report, fill Patient Name, Mobile Number, Email Address (all required), optional UHID, and Department (required — the list is configured in Patient Ticket Settings). Attach any supporting files within the configured limits.
  2. The system creates the Patient Ticket itself on submit — there's no direct form save by the public. The submitter can check progress later at /ticket-status using the reference given at submission.
  3. Desk side (Patient Ticket Agent): move Status forward — Open → Under Review → Appointment Confirmed → Closed/Cancelled — add replies visible to the patient, and use Internal Notes for staff-only context.
Sai Sparsh

Log a Sai Sparsh visit and follow-up call

Run a lifestyle-coaching intake and a later coaching call against it.

  1. Healthcare → Sparsh Visit → New, filled once at the first visit: work top to bottom through the tabs — habits/conditions tables, the Curry (Nutrition) / Hurry (Movement) / Worry (Stress) sections, the measurements the caregiver consents to.
  2. Tick any red-flag triage boxes that came up (Physiotherapy/Dietician/Cardiology/General Medicine/Counselling/Other) — this decides whether the person needs referral before coaching continues.
  3. Finish with the Touchpoint/Closure section, then Submit.
  4. For each later coaching call: Sparsh Follow Up → New. Record Progress on Pledge and any Primary Barrier, run the same short lifestyle check, agree a New Target Pledge and Confidence (1–10), set the Current Traffic Light, and Schedule Next Call before saving.
Buzz — Event Management

Create and publish a Buzz Event

Put an event live for ticket sales and check-in.

  1. Buzz → Buzz Event → New. Pick Team, Category, Host, and (for a physical event) Venue.
  2. Set Start/End Date and Time, Medium (In Person/Online), and add Short Description and About text.
  3. Tick Is Published? once it's ready to go live on the public site.
  4. Set up ticket types under Event Ticket Type (Title, Price, Currency, optional Max Tickets cap), and tick Is Published? on each to make it purchasable.
Education

Admit a Student

Bring a new student onto the roll.

  1. Student Admission runs the whole admission cycle for a program, including publishing an application form on the website (and, optionally, an application fee) — applicants apply through that form rather than a staff member typing them in one by one.
  2. Once admitted, students are enrolled into their Program/Courses and split into batches; multiple students can be admitted into the same program in one go.
LMS (Frappe Learning)

Create and publish an LMS Course

Put a course in front of learners.

  1. Courses → New Course → give it a Title and introduction → Create, which opens Course Settings for the remaining details (images, pricing, self-enrol vs. administrator-only enrolment, Published-On date).
  2. Add chapters, then lessons within each chapter — a lesson can carry video, text, a quiz, a PDF, a SCORM package or an assignment.
  3. Pick instructors from the Instructors field, then wait for the pill to actually appear before saving — the row is created a moment after you pick the name.
  4. Check Course Settings if a finished course still isn't showing up to learners — it only becomes visible once marked Published.
Watch out for: Saving immediately after picking an instructor name can show “Instructors is required” even though a name was just picked — this is a timing artifact (the child row takes ~1–1.5s to register), not a bug. Wait for the pill, then save.
